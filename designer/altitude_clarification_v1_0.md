## ⭐ 3 — Altitude Drift: What Designers Must Avoid

Altitude drift occurs when:

- expressive logic enters construction altitude  
- governance logic enters sandbox altitude  
- narrative altitude is mistaken for architectural altitude  
- sealed‑layer adjacency is accidentally invoked  

Drift causes:

- lineage corruption  
- schema mismatch  
- dashboard ingestion failure  
- membrane inversion  
- crosswalk instability  

This module prevents drift by defining altitude boundaries explicitly.
