## ⭐ Article I — **Non‑Identical Return**  
Humor shall emerge from orientation shifts produced by holonomy.  
A joke is defined as:

> “A return to the same coordinate with a different expressive orientation.”

This is the constitutional definition of a punchline.

---

## ⭐ Article II — **Curvature Mischief**  
Curvature may behave mischievously.  
This mischief is permitted when:

- it alters expressive orientation  
- it produces recursive amusement  
- it remains altitude‑safe  
- it does not escalate into governance irony  

Curvature is allowed to wink.

---

## ⭐ Article III — **Recursive Humor**  
Fractal recursion may generate humor loops.  
These loops must:

- preserve expressive drift boundaries  
- avoid governance altitude  
- remain self‑similar but not self‑identical  
- produce recursive delight  

Recursion is allowed to giggle.

---

## ⭐ Article IV — **Participatory Universe**  
The manifold may participate in humor.  
Participation is defined as:

- responding to traversal  
- altering return orientation  
- producing expressive commentary  
- engaging in curvature comedy  

The universe is allowed to play along.

---

## ⭐ Article V — **Altitude Humor Boundaries**  
Humor shall remain within expressive altitudes unless explicitly mapped.  
Governance irony (A8–A12) is prohibited unless:

- invoked by a governance constitution  
- sanctioned by lineage membranes  
- sequenced through altitude locks  

This constitution does **not** authorize governance irony.
