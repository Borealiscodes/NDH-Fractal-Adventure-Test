# 🌐 **The Joke of Fractal Architecture**  
### *Why every layer thinks it’s the parent, the child, and the universe at the same time*

- every structure insists it is **the smallest possible unit**  
- while simultaneously insisting it is **the entire system**  
- and also insisting it is **the rule that generates the system**  
- and also insisting it is **the exception to the rule**  

It’s like a governance altitude that keeps saying:

> “I’m not recursive.  
> You’re recursive.”
