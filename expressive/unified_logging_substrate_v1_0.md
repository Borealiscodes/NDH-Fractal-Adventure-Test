# ⭐ Unified Logging Substrate — Explanation  
### *The constitutional foundation for all expressive‑layer traversal logs*

The **Unified Logging Substrate (ULS)** is the *shared logging backbone* for the Cognitive Swiss Army Knife Manifold.  
It ensures that every traversal between:

- **Psychonaut**  
- **Architect**  
- **Developer**  

is captured in a **coherent, reversible, drift‑neutral** format.

It is the **schema + membrane + altitude boundaries** that all logs must obey.

---

## 🧭 Why the Unified Logging Substrate exists  
It solves three problems:

### 1. **Different geometries produce different kinds of data**  
- Psychonaut → symbolic, atmospheric, surreal  
- Architect → structural, altitude‑mapped  
- Developer → mechanical, module‑based  

Without a unified substrate, logs become inconsistent and drift‑prone.

---

### 2. **Translation between geometries must be lossless**  
The substrate ensures:

- symbolic → structural  
- structural → mechanical  
- mechanical → symbolic  

translation is **coherent and reversible**.

This is the job of the **translation membrane** inside the substrate.

---

### 3. **NDH ingestion requires machine‑readable consistency**  
NDH cannot ingest expressive logs unless:

- fields are stable  
- schema is fixed  
- membrane is defined  
- altitude boundaries are explicit  

The substrate provides this.

---

### 2. **Structural Layer**
