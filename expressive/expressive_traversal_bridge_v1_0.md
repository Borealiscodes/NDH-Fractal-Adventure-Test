### 🧭 Expressive Traversal Bridge v1.0  
**Modes bridged:** Psychonaut ↔ Architect ↔ Developer  
**Style:** Unified (constitutional + atmospheric + translation membrane)

---

#### 1. Purpose

- **Unify:** Connect the three modes without collapsing them.  
- **Stabilize:** Remove wobble when switching modes.  
- **Translate:** Ensure symbolic ↔ structural ↔ mechanical transitions stay coherent.  
- **Protect:** Prevent expressive drift while exploring higher‑altitude governance artifacts.

---

#### 2. High‑level structure

- **Constitutional Bridge:**  
  - Defines allowed transitions and reversibility.  
  - Psychonaut → Architect → Developer  
  - Psychonaut ← Architect ← Developer  

- **Atmospheric Bridge:**  
  - Wraps each transition in a felt geometry:  
    - fog → lattice → machinery  
  - Keeps the “mood” of each mode intact.

- **Translation Membrane:**  
  - Maps:  
    - symbolic → structural  
    - structural → mechanical  
    - mechanical → symbolic  
  - Ensures no information is lost or distorted when crossing.
